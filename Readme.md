### 🙋‍♂️ A Little About Me

I'm a **GenAI Developer based in New Delhi, India** with 3 years of hands-on experience. Honestly, I just love building things with AI. 

My thing? I take the latest models, frameworks, and ideas and turn them into **real, working AI agents, apps, and experiments** that people can actually use and learn from. Nothing fancy. Just practical, open-source stuff built with curiosity and a lot of coffee ☕.

I believe the best way to learn AI is to **build in public**. Every experiment, every half-baked idea, every "wait, can AI actually do this?" moment ends up as a repo on my profile. Feel free to explore, fork, break things, and build on top of what I've made. That's literally why it's all here. 🙌

When I'm not coding, I'm probably reading about the latest LLM drop, geeking out on agent architectures, or thinking about what to build next.

### 🚀 What I Build

I work across three flavours of AI:

🤖 **AI Agents** are autonomous agents that think, plan, and act. From simple tool-calling bots to multi-agent pipelines, I love exploring how far we can push LLMs to do real work on their own.

🛠️ **AI Applications** are end-to-end apps powered by LLMs — RAG systems, chatbots, document intelligence tools, and more. Built with FastAPI, Streamlit, LangChain, LlamaIndex and friends.

🧪 **Experiments & Demos** are quick, fun proof-of-concepts that explore a new model, technique, or use case. Sometimes they're polished. Sometimes they're chaotic. Always worth checking out.

> All of it is open source. All of it is yours to learn from. That's the whole point. ❤️
